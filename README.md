# airbnb-dashboard

An interactive Airbnb Performance Dashboard created using Microsoft Power BI to analyze listing growth, market share, pricing, customer ratings, reviews, seasonality, and host trust indicators.

📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides insights into Airbnb performance across:

Listing growth over time
Market share by city
Property type pricing
Customer ratings
Review frequency
Monthly seasonality
Host verification and trust signals

📌 Key KPIs
Total Listings	279,712

Cities	10

Property Types	144

Reviews	5.37M

Unique Hosts	182,024

💡 Key Insights
Paris, New York, and Sydney account for almost half of the total listings and 48% of total reviews.
Hotel rooms have the highest average price at $800, followed by entire places at $673.
Private rooms have the lowest displayed average price at $462.
Customer ratings are generally high across the analyzed cities and rating categories.
98% of customers wrote reviews three times or fewer.
The dashboard analyzes monthly review activity to identify seasonal patterns.
Nearly two-thirds of Airbnb hosts are fully verified, according to the dashboard.
